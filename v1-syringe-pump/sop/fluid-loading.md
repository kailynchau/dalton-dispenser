# Fluid Loading SOP

## SU-8

**Equipment**
- Capillary to be used during dispensing
- Wipes
- 1 mL syringe
- SU-8 packaging
- Blunt needles for syringe
- Petri dish

**Procedure**
1. Wear appropriate safety equipment (nitrile gloves and mask).
2. Turn the carbon filter fan on and place the wipes in front of it.
3. Attach the blunt needle to the 1 mL syringe and remove the SU-8 bottle from its packaging.
4. Open the SU-8 bottle in front of the carbon filter fan and draw the desired amount into the syringe. Close the bottle and return it to the packaging.
5. Remove the blunt needle from the syringe and replace it with the capillary.
6. Holding the syringe over a petri dish, slowly dispense fluid into the capillary until it's filled, avoiding or eliminating air bubbles.
7. Remove the loaded capillary from the syringe and attach it to the dispensing head.
8. Put all waste in the SU-8 waste bucket.

## PDMS – High Viscosity (Dowsil SE 1700)

**Equipment**
- 5 mL syringe
- 1 mL syringe
- Luer-Lock syringe caps
- Wipes
- Syringe rack
- Coffee mixing sticks (or similar)
- Digital scale
- PDMS packaging

**Procedure**
1. Wear appropriate safety equipment (nitrile gloves and mask).
2. Open the 5 mL syringe, remove the plunger, and put a syringe cap on it.
3. Place the syringe in the syringe rack and put the rack on the digital scale.
4. Zero the scale.
5. Remove the clear base from the PDMS packaging and open the container.
6. Use the mixing sticks to move the desired amount of base into the syringe (the team normally used 3 g). *Tip: use the plunger to push base deeper if it gathers near the top.*
7. Weigh the PDMS base.
8. Close the base container and return it to the packaging.
9. Remove the catalyst from the packaging and open its container.
10. Load the 1 mL syringe with catalyst and add **10% of the base weight** to the 5 mL syringe (e.g. 3 g base → 0.3 g catalyst).
11. Thoroughly mix base and catalyst with the mixing sticks.
12. Use the plunger to push the mixture to the bottom of the syringe. *Tip: loosening the cap slightly makes this easier — retighten when done.*
13. Wipe off any PDMS on the outside of the syringe. Close the catalyst container and return it to the packaging.
14. Attach the loaded syringe to the dispensing head using the syringe holder and its two M3 screws.
15. Put all waste in the PDMS waste bucket.
